## 3. Privacy & Visibility Settings — Your Rights and Controls

> This section explains the privacy controls available to you in **Settings → Privacy & Visibility** and the legal framework governing them. Full technical specification: [PrivacyVisibilitySpec.md](PrivacyVisibilitySpec.md).

### 3.1 Principle of Privacy by Default

By default, BestMe applies the **most privacy-protective settings** available (GDPR Art.25 — Privacy by Default and Privacy by Design). You may choose to make more information visible, but we will never default to exposing your personal data without your explicit action.

---

### 3.2 Account Privacy (Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Account Privacy)

| Setting | Default | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| **Private account** | OFF (open profile) | When ON, only approved followers see your content. Lawful for 18+ under GDPR Art.25. |
| **Profile in search results** | ON | Whether your profile appears in BestMe's internal search. You may opt out at any time (GDPR Art.17). |
| **SEO indexing** | **OFF (cannot be changed to ON by default)** | Whether search engines (Google, Bing) can index your profile. **Permanently OFF by default per GDPR Art.25.** You may opt in. |
| **Relationship status visibility** | Friends only | Who can see your displayed relationship status. |

> 🔴 **GDPR Art.25 obligation:** `seo_indexable` is set to `false` by default and cannot be changed to a default of `true` by the platform. Violation carries fines up to €10 million or 4% of global turnover.

### 3.3 Profile Visibility (Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Profile Visibility)

You control who can see each element of your public profile:

| Profile field | Default visibility |

| Full name | Everyone |

> ⚠️ **Birthday protection (GDPR Art.9):** Your full date of birth is classified as sensitive personal data. By default, only the **age** (not the full date) is visible, and only to friends. You may restrict this further but not expose the full date publicly without explicit action.

### 3.6 Interactions (Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Interactions)

You control who can interact with you on BestMe:

| Interaction | Default | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Who can send you messages** | Friends | DSA Art.14 · Israel PPL §2 |
| **Who can tag you** | Friends | GDPR Art.25 |

### 3.7 Discoverability (Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Discoverability)

| Setting | Default | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Opt out of algorithmic recommendations** | OFF (participating) | GDPR Art.22 · DSA Art.29 · CCPA §1798.121 |
| **"Why am I recommended this?" transparency** | **ON** | DSA Art.27 — algorithmic transparency |

> **Your right to opt out:** Under GDPR Art.22 and DSA Art.29, you have the right to object to fully automated decisions affecting you, including algorithmic recommendations. You may disable recommendation participation at any time via Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Discoverability.  
> **Algorithmic transparency:** You may tap "Why am I recommended this?" on any recommended content or profile to receive an explanation of the factors used. This is provided by default per DSA Art.27.

### 3.8 Safety & Blocked Accounts (Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Safety & Blocked Accounts)

**Blocking:** You may block any user. Blocked users cannot see your profile, send you messages, or interact with your content. The blocking is invisible to the blocked user — they will see a neutral "profile not found" message (GDPR anti-harassment principle).

**Restricting:** You may apply a soft restriction to a user. They will still see your public posts, but their comments will only be visible to themselves (like Instagram's "Restrict" feature). Useful for managing harassment without escalation.

**Reporting:** You may report any user or content that violates our Community Guidelines or applicable law:
- Three-dot menu (⋯) on any profile → **Report**
- Three-dot menu (⋯) on any post, comment, or photo → **Report**
- Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Safety & Blocked Accounts → Report a user

> 🔴 **DSA Art.16 obligation (mandatory for EU):** BestMe is legally required to provide a notice-and-action mechanism for illegal content. The Report function is mandatory and cannot be removed or disabled. Absence of a reporting mechanism would result in rejection from the App Store (EU) and Google Play (EU) and constitutes a violation of DSA Art.16.
